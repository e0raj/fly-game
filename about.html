About

A tiny arcade game

Fly is a small arcade game that fits in one HTML file. No engine, no libraries, no image files, no audio files. Every character, cloud, pipe and sound you see and hear is drawn or generated in code while it runs.

What is inside

  • Four characters, drawn procedurally — a bird with beating wings, a rocket with a live flame, a fluttering paper plane and an auto-rickshaw with spinning wheels.
  • A full-screen world that adapts to any screen without letterboxing or stretched art.
  • Flight physics on a fixed 240 Hz timestep, so it feels identical on a 60 Hz phone and a 144 Hz monitor.
  • Seamlessly repeating scenery — parallax hills, a tree line, drifting clouds and a scrolling ground.
  • A sky that moves from day to sunset to night as your score climbs.
  • Sound synthesised live with the Web Audio API.
  • Three difficulty modes, a local top five, and a best score saved on your device.
