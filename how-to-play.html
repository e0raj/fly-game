The idea

You fly, the world scrolls, and pipes get in the way. Tap to lift, let go to sink, and thread the gaps. Touch a pipe or the ground and the run ends.

Flying

The flight is deliberately floaty, not twitchy. One tap lifts you about 72 units and settles back in just under a second, so a tap roughly every second holds your height. A full-screen drop takes about two seconds, which means you always have time to react.

The nose tips up as you tap and noses over slowly as you sink — so the character glides rather than falls.

Scoring

  • One point for clearing a pipe.
  • Two points and a chime for threading it dead centre — a reward for flying with control.
  • Your best score and a top five are kept on your device.
  • Medals: Bronze at 5, Silver at 12, Gold at 25, Platinum at 40.

Characters

Bird, Rocket, Paper plane and Auto-rickshaw. All four fly on exactly the same physics and share the same hitbox, so the game is never easier or harder depending on who you pick — only the look, the tilt and the trail change. Swipe left or right on the start screen to flick between them.

Difficulty

CHILLWide gaps, slow pipes
NORMALThe standard run
INSANETight gaps, fast pipes

Speed also climbs as your score grows, in every mode.

The sky

You start in bright daylight. Around twenty points the sky warms into sunset, and past thirty the sun sinks, the moon rises and the stars come out. Keep flying to see the whole day pass.
