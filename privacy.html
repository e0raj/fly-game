Privacy

Short version: nothing leaves your device

Fly has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no server. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to send anywhere. The game is a static page that runs entirely in your browser.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a few small preferences in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • Your best score
  • Your top five scores, with the date each was set
  • Your chosen character, difficulty and sound setting

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data, or the site data for this page, removes all of it. There is no way for anyone — including me — to read it remotely.

Links out

The only links that leave this site are to Instagram and GitHub. They open in a new tab when you tap them, and from that point the privacy policy of those sites applies instead of this one.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children either. It shows no ads and has no chat or social features.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example if a leaderboard or any kind of analytics is added — this page will be updated first and the version number below will move.
